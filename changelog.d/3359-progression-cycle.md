# Drafted trainings follow the team's cycle, and say when the cycle is paused (#3359)

Where a team has a cycle, the planner now takes the week's intensity from it
rather than from the macro-block alone. A week with a game is planned at neutral
intensity, and the draft says why — "there is a game this week, so the cycle is
paused" — instead of quietly coming out flat.

A team with no cycle plans exactly as it did before.

One long-standing silence also gets a voice: a macro-block that runs longer than
its weekly profile has always planned its later weeks at neutral intensity
without saying so. It still does, but now it tells you the profile ran out and
that adding weeks to it keeps the progression going.
